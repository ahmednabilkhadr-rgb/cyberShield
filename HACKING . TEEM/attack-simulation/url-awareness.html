KNOW THE THREAT

What is URL Spoofing?

URL spoofing is a deceptive technique in which a link or domain is designed to look similar to a legitimate website. It may trick visitors into trusting the wrong destination.

01

Look-alike Domains

A domain may use a name that resembles a trusted organization.

02

Suspicious Subdomains

The real registered domain may appear at the end of a long address.

03

Unexpected Links

Unrequested messages containing links deserve extra attention.
